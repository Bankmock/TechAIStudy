South Indian Movie Search

South Indian cinema includes major industries from Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, Kerala and Karnataka. Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam and Kannada movies have their own unique storytelling traditions, stars, music and filmmaking styles.

Modern audiences increasingly watch Indian movies across language boundaries. A Telugu movie can become popular among Hindi-speaking audiences, while Tamil, Malayalam and Kannada films can attract viewers throughout India and internationally.
